The 9 core EFT techniques that form the foundation of clinical practice. You'll learn the science, the protocols, and how to apply them effectively to your own emotional and physical issues. This is the essential foundation for working with anyone else.

Accreditation and certification

On completing the training, assignments, and documented case studies, you'll receive your EFT Practitioner Certification, recognised by the International Institute of Complementary Therapists (IICT).
